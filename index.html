Ask our team
Who does Affinity Steps work with?

Affinity Steps focuses on B2B recruitment outsourcing and RPO delivery for staffing and recruitment agencies, particularly US staffing firms.

What recruitment tasks can your team support?

Candidate sourcing, research, engagement, qualification, talent intelligence, recruitment operations, ATS/database management and business development research.

Do you work inside our ATS or CRM?

The delivery model is designed around your existing systems and agreed workflow. Specific access and tools are agreed before work begins.

Where is the delivery team based?

The delivery team is based in Udaipur, India, and works around the local business hours agreed with the client.

How do the five free candidate profiles work?
